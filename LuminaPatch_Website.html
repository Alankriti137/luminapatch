The Problem

Medicine watches episodically.
Cancer grows continuously.

BRCA-positive patients and breast cancer survivors have no continuous monitoring between clinical appointments. Current surveillance is entirely episodic — a mammogram once a year, an MRI every 6 months at best.


Between those appointments, breast tissue is completely unwatched. If a tumour begins forming the day after a clear MRI, it will not be detected for up to 6 months. During that window it can progress from Stage 1 to Stage 3 — and the difference in survival is catastrophic.


No wearable, no continuous monitor, no at-home surveillance tool of any kind exists for breast cancer monitoring. That is the status quo.

The Solution

A wearable patch that watches your tissue every single day.

LuminaPatch uses four completely independent sensing channels simultaneously. A personalised AI model learns each patient's individual tissue signature over two weeks, then monitors continuously for sustained multi-channel shifts consistent with early tumour development. All four channels must agree before any alert is generated — keeping false positives low.

💡
NIR Spectroscopy
Near-infrared light penetrates breast tissue up to 8cm, detecting haemoglobin concentration changes driven by early tumour angiogenesis — new blood vessel formation that feeds developing tumours weeks before any mass is visible on imaging.
⚡
Bioimpedance
A low-amplitude alternating current sweeps across multiple frequencies, measuring how tissue conducts electricity. Cancerous cells disrupt normal tissue architecture in ways that shift the electrical impedance profile measurably and consistently.
🌡️
Thermal Sensing
Developing tumours have elevated metabolic activity and recruit abnormal blood vessel networks — both of which cause local tissue temperature to run 0.5–2°C warmer than surrounding healthy tissue. A thermistor array detects this pattern passively.
🧬
Syn Bio Layer
Engineered microorganisms in a biocompatible hydrogel detect cancer-associated volatile organic compounds secreted through the skin above early tumours — adding a molecular biological detection channel that electronics alone cannot replicate.
Personalised AI — not population averages.
Every existing tool compares a patient to population averages. LuminaPatch compares each patient to herself — her own baseline established over two weeks of continuous monitoring. A change that is normal for one person and abnormal for another only becomes distinguishable when you compare someone to herself, not to a statistical average built from millions of different people.
How It Works

Four steps. No clinic visit required.

LuminaPatch is designed to fit into a patient's life with minimal friction. The patch is prescribed by an oncologist or genetic counsellor and worn like a plaster under clothing.

01
Apply
The flexible patch is placed directly on the chest over the area of concern. No special adhesive, no medical procedure, no clinic visit required. It connects to the patient's phone automatically.
02
Learn
Over the first two weeks, the patch learns the patient's personal tissue baseline across their normal daily patterns — menstrual cycle, hydration, exercise, and sleep. This personalisation is what makes the system clinically meaningful.
03
Monitor
All four channels run continuously in the background. The patient goes about their life normally. The app logs data silently and the patient can check their personal timeline at any time and add their own notes.
04
Alert
When all four channels shift together in a cancer-consistent pattern for 72+ consecutive hours, the patient receives a calm, informative notification. They review their data, add context if they want, and choose whether to share a structured clinical report with their doctor.

Technical Depth

All components exist today.

LuminaPatch integrates four validated sensing modalities on a single flexible substrate. The technical gap between current capabilities and a fully functional device is under five years.

Physical Construction
Flexible PDMS substrate with serpentine interconnects
The patch is built on polydimethylsiloxane — a biocompatible, skin-conforming material. Serpentine wiring allows the substrate to bend without breaking circuit connections, enabling comfortable continuous wear.
NIR System
700–900nm multi-wavelength LED array
Multiple LED wavelengths optimised for differential absorption of haemoglobin and lipids. A 2025 study from Simon Fraser University and BC Cancer demonstrated 94.7% sensitivity and 84.2% specificity in distinguishing healthy from malignant breast tissue using NIR DOT.
Bioimpedance System
Multi-frequency AC sweep with AD5933 chip
Tetrapolar electrode configuration minimises contact impedance artefacts. Frequency sweep from 1kHz to 1MHz extracts Cole-Cole parameters — a validated mathematical model of tissue electrical behaviour that changes characteristically with malignancy.
AI Architecture
LSTM personalised baseline model on ESP32
Lightweight time-series anomaly detection running on-device. Compares today's readings to the patient's personal established baseline. Alert threshold requires all four channels to exceed their individual thresholds simultaneously for 72+ consecutive hours.
